# Changelog

## 0.1.0
- First public web release on itch.io.
- Eleven floors (B1 Intake to B11 WARDEN Core) with a boss on every floor: Crusher, Dynamo, Matriarch, Assembler and WARDEN.
- Enemy ranks (novice, journeyman, master) with XP that adds to the score; poison spiders; steam vents and electric plates with clear warnings.
- Secrets are taken from the dead, with a poem and the gain shown first. Terminals show gameplay tips.
- Keyboard-only controls, an alarm shown by the pulse monitor, retry from the start of the floor when you die.
- Intro cutscene, ending cutscene, end report and credits. English and Turkish.
