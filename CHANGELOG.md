# Changelog

## 0.1.1
- New Exile: the main character is redrawn from a 3D model, with larger and smoother walk and aim frames.
- WARDEN now talks to you between fights: twelve voiced lines, at most two per floor.
- A larger exit beacon with a distance readout replaces the small exit arrow.
- Loading bar on startup.
- Trap hints explain the cycle and repeat once per floor when a trap hits you; a reminder appears when only a few hostiles are left.
- Long hints and subtitles wrap instead of running under the map.
- More machines on every floor, with more ammo, stims, crates and barrels to match.
- Machines resist steam and electricity: traps now do them half damage.
- Turkish voices re-rendered with a natural Turkish voice; WARDEN now speaks through a distant loudspeaker.
- Quieter footsteps, fuller heartbeat.

## 0.1.0
- First public web release on itch.io.
- Eleven floors (B1 Intake to B11 WARDEN Core) with a boss on every floor: Crusher, Dynamo, Matriarch, Assembler and WARDEN.
- Enemy ranks (novice, journeyman, master) with XP that adds to the score; poison spiders; steam vents and electric plates with clear warnings.
- Secrets are taken from the dead, with a poem and the gain shown first. Terminals show gameplay tips.
- Keyboard-only controls, an alarm shown by the pulse monitor, retry from the start of the floor when you die.
- Intro cutscene, ending cutscene, end report and credits. English and Turkish.
