# Station Zero 0.1.1: WARDEN speaks

> *"If I am the monster, ask who wrote me."*

0.1.1 is a small update built from the first round of feedback. The Exile has a new look, the floors are busier, the exit is easier to find, and WARDEN finally has something to say.

## What is new

- **A new Exile.** The main character is redrawn. The sprite is larger, and walking and aiming read more clearly in the dark.
- **WARDEN talks to you.** Between fights it speaks, in its own voice, about the people who sent you. Twelve lines, at most two per floor, never the same one twice in a run.
- **A proper exit beacon.** The small arrow is gone. A larger marker with a distance readout points at the hatch and sits above it when it is on screen.
- **Busier floors.** Every floor has more drones, spiders and tanks, and more ammo, stims, crates and barrels to deal with them.
- **Traps explain themselves.** Steam vents and electric plates run on a cycle: hum, fire, rest. The hint now says so, and it comes back once per floor if a trap catches you.
- **Machines resist the traps.** Steam and electricity now do them half damage, so luring a drone over a vent helps but no longer does the job for you.
- **"Few hostiles left."** When only three machines remain, the game tells you, and reminds you that the hatch stays sealed until they are gone.
- **Loading bar** on startup.
- **New Turkish voices.** The Turkish lines were re-rendered with a natural Turkish voice. WARDEN now reaches you through a distant loudspeaker.
- **Sound.** Footsteps are quieter. The heartbeat is heavier.

## Fixes

- Long hints and subtitles ran under the map. They now wrap and stay clear of it.
- Switching language in the menu now loads the voices for that language.
- Menu panels size themselves correctly around long text.

## What I would like to know

- Do WARDEN's lines land, or do they get in the way?
- Is the exit beacon clear without being loud?
- Are the fuller floors better, or too much?

Please leave feedback in the comments and mention the version shown in the bottom-right of the main menu (v0.1.1). Thank you for playing.
